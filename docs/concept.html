Polymetis concept

A dark grey 60-key HHKB Professional 2 layout with blank low-profile caps coloured by finger, with modifiers in the body colour. The case outline, key positions and stack come from the design files and Gateron's drawings; the case finish and keycap shape are illustrative.

Height stack

Plate, gap, PCB and socket figures are from Gateron's drawings. The frame is 5.3 mm: the 1.3 mm gap, the 1.6 mm PCB and the tallest part underneath (the 2.16 mm flash chip), plus clearance.
