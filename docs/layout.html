Polymetis layout

HHKB Professional 2 layout: Backspace sits one row lower (on the Tab row), \ and ` move up to the number row, Control takes the Caps Lock spot, and the bottom row has one 6.25u spacebar. Each key is coloured by the finger that presses it.

Modifier keys:

Drawn to scale (1u = 19.05 mm). The solid line marks the centre of the space bar; the dotted line marks the centre of the case.

How symmetric it is

The bottom row is mirror-image: Alt, Cmd, Space, Cmd, Alt, with the same key sizes on both sides.

It is centred on your hands rather than on the case. The space bar's centre (6.875u from the left) is where the boundary between the left and right index fingers falls, averaged over the three letter rows (T|Y 6.5u, G|H 6.75u, B|N 7.25u). Centring it on the case instead would put it 0.6u to the right of that boundary and would also put the right-hand keys on top of the reset pad next to the RP2040.

The letter rows are HHKB-standard, so the right little finger covers more keys than the left (15 against 8). That comes from the HHKB layout itself.

Key sizes

  • 51 × 1u letters, numbers, punctuation, Esc, Fn, Alt
  • 4 × 1.5u Tab, Backspace, both Cmd
  • 2 × 1.75u Control, right Shift
  • 2 × 2.25u Enter, left Shift
  • 1 × 6.25u Space

Bottom row: 1.25u gap, Alt 1u, Cmd 1.5u, Space 6.25u, Cmd 1.5u, Alt 1u, 2.5u gap. Full row width is 15u.
